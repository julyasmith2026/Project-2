Mini Biography : Dr. Marie Curie

Dr. Marie Curie was a physicist and chemist famous for her pioneering research on radioactivity.

Early Life

She was born in Warsaw, Poland, in 1867. Despite facing difficulties in her education, she continued learning and later studied in Paris, France.

Important Achievement

She was the first woman to win a Nobel Prize and the first person to win Nobel Prizes in two different scientific fields.

She made remarkable contributions to the study of radioactivity.

Her discoveries of polonium and radium changed the development of modern science.

Note: Marie Curie remains an inspiration to scientists and students around the world.

She was only a physicist. She was also a chemist and a Nobel Prize-winning researcher.

Chemistry includes compounds such as H2O and mathematical expressions such as 102.
